Mediterranean / THE DREAMER

A VISUAL STORY

A shared sea, different livelihoods. Blue Future follows work and lives tied to the Mediterranean.

01 / FROM THE FIELD

More than a horizon.

Seen from above, the coast becomes a meeting of rock, shallows and deep water. Seen from shore, it is also a place of work, knowledge and daily routines. These supplied photographs give the sea a human scale.

02 / FROM THE FIELD

Three perspectives, one sea.

Blue Future is a collective documentary about sustainable livelihoods around the Mediterranean. WWF and COGITO are credited in its production. Rabii Ben Brahim appears among the four filmmaker credits, alongside Ante Gugić, Emanuele Quartarone and Beatrice Surano.

03 / FROM THE FIELD

People belong in the frame.

The documentary’s environmental context includes the people whose work is connected to the sea. The coastal footage and photographs on this page extend that theme; they are selected from the supplied archive and are not presented as verified production stills from Blue Future.
