Tunisia / THE DREAMER

A VISUAL STORY

A journey across Tunisia for the Land Rover Defender campaign, with The Dreamer at the centre of the film.

01 / FROM THE FIELD

A different way to travel.

The road links coast, dunes and open ground. In this campaign, the journey is the setting for Rabii’s identity as The Dreamer: someone drawn to exploration and the places beyond the familiar view.

02 / FROM THE FIELD

Exploration in context.

YKONE’s published case study identifies the Land Rover Defender campaign. The existing site connects the work with a road trip across Tunisia. The local clip shown here is supplied field footage, distinct from the linked campaign film.

03 / FROM THE FIELD

Making room for the landscape.

Vehicle, traveller and place share the frame. The strongest moments let the surroundings carry the story: an open road, a pause beside the vehicle and another stretch of country ahead.
