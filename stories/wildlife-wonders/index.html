WILDLIFE / CONSERVATION

A world worth looking closer at.

Tunisia / THE DREAMER

A VISUAL STORY

From the smallest encounter to the wider landscape, Wildlife Wonders brings Tunisia’s natural heritage into view.

01 / FROM THE FIELD

The living world, close up.

An owl in the shelter of stone. A reptile resting in sunlight. A bird in flight. Small encounters are a reminder that a landscape is more than its scenery. The camera gives those lives time and space within the frame.

02 / FROM THE FIELD

A story made in the field.

Rabii describes working with wildlife ecologist Zakher Bouragaoui through the Tunisian Wonders initiative. The resulting film, Wildlife Wonders, follows a purpose central to The Dreamer: bringing Tunisia’s natural heritage into view.

03 / FROM THE FIELD

Attention is a beginning.

The film connects visual storytelling with an interest in the living world. Its contribution here is an invitation to notice, to learn and to look again. No numerical conservation outcome is claimed for this project.
