EXPEDITION / PHOTOGRAPHIC ESSAY

Between sea & stone.

Djerba, Tunisia / THE DREAMER

A VISUAL STORY

Whitewashed courtyards, blue doorways and a filming day by the coast. A journey through the supplied Djerba field archive.

01 / FROM THE FIELD

A place in the details.

The visual story begins in the architecture: the curve of a courtyard, bright walls under open sky, and the blue of a weathered doorway. Together, these details hold a sense of place without needing a grand panorama.

02 / FROM THE FIELD

On the other side of the camera.

The field photographs bring the filmmaking process into view. Rabii works with a camera, people gather for conversation, and the crew shares the space around them. The story of a place is also the story of meeting it.

03 / FROM THE FIELD

The coast keeps moving.

A short coastal film gives the still photographs another rhythm. The supplied material is presented as a field essay, rather than a claim about a commissioned campaign or measurable conservation outcome.
