Tunisia / THE DREAMER

A VISUAL STORY

Sharing the camera, sharing attention. Photography becomes a meeting point between people and place.

01 / FROM THE FIELD

The camera can be a meeting point.

In the supplied photographs, participants gather around the process of making an image. Watching, talking and taking turns become part of the story. The camera is a practical way to share attention.

02 / FROM THE FIELD

A place through its people.

The existing project archive describes Rabii’s participation in the CinémaJet photography programme in Zaghouan. The activity invites a view of the city through the residents who give it life.

03 / FROM THE FIELD

Beyond a finished photograph.

The images on this page document conversation and participation. They show a process, rather than a quantified impact claim. The relationships around the frame matter alongside the photograph itself.
