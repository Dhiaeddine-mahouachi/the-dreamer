Tunisia / THE DREAMER

A VISUAL STORY

From forest to coast and open country: a cinematic journey through Tunisia.

01 / FROM THE FIELD

Follow the landscape.

The Tunisia film moves between wide vistas and the details that make them particular. Its landscape frames are gathered here as a visual essay, keeping the distinction between moving-image stills and standalone photography clear.

02 / FROM THE FIELD

Let the image breathe.

A coastline, a hillside and the light across open ground each ask for a different pace. In the film, movement links those views. On the page, space between the images lets each one stand on its own.

03 / FROM THE FIELD

Another way to see home.

Tunisia remains at the heart of The Dreamer’s work. The journey is an invitation to discover the country through its landscape, its living world and the people connected to it.
