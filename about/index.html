Curiosity became a journey.

Rabii has spoken about watching documentaries with his father and spending time near the forest with his uncle. Those early experiences shaped the stories he would choose to tell. He began exploring Tunisia with a camera and a backpack.

Through The Dreamer, film and photography become a way to draw attention to Tunisia’s natural and cultural heritage — its forests, coast, open landscapes, wildlife and the people connected to them.
